# Managing the website

Sign in at **yourdomain/admin**. Every change you save appears on the website within seconds.

## Daily: enquiries and bookings

**Enquiries and bookings** lists every contact message and consultation request, newest first. New ones are highlighted in gold.

1. Open an enquiry to see the details. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp** to reach the client directly.
2. Change the status as you go: *new → contacted → scheduled → completed*, or *closed*. Mark junk as *spam*.
3. Add internal notes (only staff see them), then **Update enquiry**.
4. **Export CSV** downloads everything for Excel or Google Sheets.

## Adding testimonials

Only publish feedback the client has agreed to share.

1. **Testimonials → New testimonial.** Choose the type: written, WhatsApp screenshot or video.
2. Enter the name exactly as the client agreed to be shown. Initials are fine.
3. For WhatsApp screenshots, **blur phone numbers, profile pictures and anyone else's name before uploading.**
4. Tick *The client has given written permission* and note how (for example “WhatsApp message, 12 Oct 2026”).
5. Set status to **Published**. The system will not publish without the permission tick.

Client stories work the same way, with three parts: where the client started, the guidance given, and the outcome in the client's own words. Avoid phrases that promise results.

## Writing articles

1. **Blog posts → New post.** The URL slug fills in from the title.
2. Write in the editor. Use **Heading** for main sections; they automatically become the article's table of contents.
3. Add a cover image and describe it in *Cover alt text* for visually impaired readers.
4. To publish later, choose a future date and status **Scheduled**.
5. *SEO title* and *SEO description* are optional; if blank, the title and excerpt are used.

## The About page

**Profile** holds the sections of the About page. Sections that contain placeholders are marked unverified and stay hidden. When you have the real information (qualifications, certifications, achievements, experience), replace the placeholder text, tick *I have verified every fact in this section*, and save. **Career timeline** entries appear once at least one is published.

## Programmes

**Programmes** holds the student, parent and teacher programmes (Exam Ready From the Inside, Student Inner Excellence, Child Script Coach, Handwriting Mastery, and the teacher consultation). Each has a short summary, three pillars, what it includes, who it is for, a price note, the button text, an optional WhatsApp keyword (for example COACH) and an optional flyer image. Programmes also appear in the booking form.

## Drafts waiting for approval

Some content from the flyers is saved as drafts so nothing goes public without approval: one testimonial, the Instagram link and 13 flyers in the gallery. See `docs/CONTENT-TO-CONFIRM.md`.

## Photos and videos

**Gallery → New photo:** upload, choose a category, and write alt text describing the picture. **Videos:** paste a YouTube link or upload a file.

## Other settings

- **Website settings** (admins): logo, portrait, hero text, short biography, hours.
- **SEO overrides:** change the search title or description of any page by its path, for example `/about`.
- **Social media:** add profile links; icons appear in the footer.
- **Legal pages** (admins): edit policies after legal review.
- **Users and roles** (admins): give colleagues *editor* access to manage content, or *viewer* for read-only.

Deleting is limited to admins and cannot be undone. Changing a service or article URL slug changes its web address; tell your developer so an old-address redirect can be added.
